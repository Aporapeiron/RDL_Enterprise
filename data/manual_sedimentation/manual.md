# Synthetic IT Support Manual

This synthetic manual describes password reset, Wi-Fi setup, VPN access, and privileged-access requests. The benchmark varies only how much of this same manual is encoded in the runtime graph.

- D1 Procedure: direct procedures
- D2 Conditions: procedures plus applicability and exclusion conditions
- D3 Relational: procedures, conditions, dependencies, exceptions, and policy context

The expected outcomes are stored separately from runtime inputs in `cases.json`.
